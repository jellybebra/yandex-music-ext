# Privacy Policy for Yandex Music - Classic Vibe Player

**Last Updated:** June 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle user data in the **Yandex Music - Classic Vibe Player** browser extension (the "Extension").

## 1. No Data Collection
We believe in absolute privacy. The Extension **does not collect, store, track, or transmit** any personal data, usage metrics, browsing history, or identifiers.

## 2. No Third-Party Sharing
Since we do not collect any information, we do not share, sell, or disclose any user data to third parties.

## 3. Local Operation
All modifications of the Yandex Music page layout and settings are performed entirely locally on your device within your web browser. The Extension stores only your local on/off preference for the classic design. No data is sent to external servers.

## 4. Permissions Used
*   Yandex Music site access is used solely to run the local `inject.js` script and restore the classic design.
*   The `storage` permission saves the on/off preference locally in the browser.
*   The `scripting` permission enables or disables `inject.js` according to that preference.

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via GitHub or at your publisher contact email.
